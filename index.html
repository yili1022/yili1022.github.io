About

I’m a senior design student at the School of Visual Arts (SVA) in New York, with a strong focus on branding, visual identity, and UI design. My work centers on building clear visual systems and thoughtful digital experiences. I’m deeply interested in how structure, typography, and interaction come together to shape perception and behavior.

I’m naturally curious and constantly exploring new tools, design methods, and emerging technologies. From visual identities to interface systems, I approach every project with both analytical thinking and aesthetic sensitivity. For me, design is not just about making things look good — it’s about creating clarity, intention, and meaningful connection. I aim to grow into a multidisciplinary designer who can think strategically, design visually, and build with purpose.

Focus: Branding · Visual Identity · UI Design · Packaging · Typography · Interaction · Creative Coding
